<p>OpenSGolf is a free, open source engine for the golf course building game Sid Meier's SimGolf (2002). Lay out holes, plant trees and dig ponds, put up a clubhouse and shops, hire staff, and watch your golfers play your course, chat, complain and come back for more.</p>

<p>It comes with its own complete set of free graphics and sounds, so you can start playing right away. If you own SimGolf, tap <b>Use my SimGolf files...</b> on the title screen and pick a .zip of your SimGolf folder or the folder itself: OpenSGolf copies the files into its own storage and then plays with the original art and sounds. No game files are included.</p>

<p>With every option at its default it aims to play exactly like the original. On top of that it offers:</p>
<ul>
<li>Touch controls made for phones and tablets, and game controller support</li>
<li>Optional enhanced, high-resolution graphics</li>
<li>Optional fixes for bugs of the original game</li>
<li>Optional interface helpers and extras, all off until you turn them on</li>
<li>A course editor for your own scenarios, and content packs (mods)</li>
</ul>

<p>The game plays in landscape. How to play, and the touch controls: https://github.com/crspytopgn/opensgolf/blob/main/docs/PLAYING.md</p>
