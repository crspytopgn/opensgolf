# How do I...?

Short answers to the questions players ask most. The full reference is [PLAYING.md](PLAYING.md); each answer links to
its section there. Something missing? Ask in the [issues](https://github.com/crspytopgn/opensgolf/issues) and it will be
added here.

## Building

**...make a scenic bridge run the other way?**
A bridge has no turn button: it lines up with the paths beside it. Lay a path on the bank at each end, on the line you
want the bridge to run, and put the bridge on the water between them. A bridge already placed turns too when you add a
path beside it. With no path beside it, it faces its default way. The direction is fixed on the map, so turning the
camera changes how it looks on screen, not where it goes.

**...turn a building before placing it?**
Press Tab while the building is under the pointer; each press turns it a quarter. (Tab does not turn bridges, see above.)

**...take something back?**
Shift-click a path, bench, flower bed, scenic tree or bridge to remove it and get its cost back. A building is
demolished with no refund. See [Buildings and garden items](PLAYING.md#buildings-and-garden-items).

**...get my buildings to earn money?**
Join them to the clubhouse with a path. A building that is not joined is not used.

**...raise or lower the land quickly?**
With a tool armed, point at the corner and press = to raise it or - to lower it.

## Tournaments and championships

**...get a tournament at my course?**
1. Wait for the SGA. Once a year, at the start of May in the game's calendar, the SGA looks at your course (while your
   pro is not out playing). If it likes what it sees, a message offers you a tournament.
2. Open the **People** panel. The rows at the top are your pro's (Gary Golf unless you renamed him); click **Begin
   Tournament** there, or press **Shift+J**.
3. The SGA checks the course again. If it fails, its report scores ten things out of 10 (length, number of holes, time
   to play, fun, variety, scenic holes, holes that test length, accuracy and imagination, and facilities joined to the
   clubhouse), so you can see what to improve before next May.
4. If it passes, untick any preparations you do not want (TV towers, smoother greens, deeper rough, tournament pars)
   and start.

No offer coming? The usual reasons are too few holes, buildings not joined to the clubhouse by a path, or your pro being
out on the course when May starts. See [SGA tournaments](PLAYING.md#sga-tournaments).

**...play my course in Play a Championship?**
In a game press **Shift+C** (or System Functions, *Save course for Championship*), and save your pro there too (System
Functions, *Save your pro for Championship*). Then on the title menu choose **Play a Championship**, pick a difficulty,
your course and your pro. It is a one-off event and does not change your saved game. See
[Play a Championship](PLAYING.md#play-a-championship).

**...share my course with a friend?**
Tick *Course sharing* in the OpenSGolf extras, then choose System Functions, *Share course (copy a course code)*. Send
your friend the code; they press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac) in OpenSGolf, or click *Paste a course code* on Play a
Championship's course list, and play it there. Your course record goes with it as the score to beat. See
[OpenSGolf extras](PLAYING.md#opensgolf-extras).

## Your pro

**...send my pro out to play?**
Open the **People** panel and click **Practice Round** in your pro's rows. **Match** lights up once a famous golfer has
challenged your club. See [Your pro](PLAYING.md#your-pro-gary-golf).
